How we work in Utah

Utah is full site management plus on-site service by arrangement. The Wasatch Front (Salt Lake City, Ogden, Provo) and St. George have different water rules and different winters. Neither is an on-site territory.

Own a wash in Utah? Call or text (619) 704-7719, Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM PT. We aim to respond quickly. Request service.

On-site repair is centered on Southern California. Outside California, across the Southwest United States and Hawaii, Car Wash Management provides full site management and on-site service by arrangement. Travel charges apply to on-site visits; we will tell you before we book.

St. George’s gallons-per-car rule

St. George City Code section 10-17A-15 requires car washes to be plumbed for wash-water and RO-reject recycling when they are built. For the window from July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2027, the code says a car wash is not permitted unless it averages 35 gallons or less of culinary water per car, and the recycling system has to be installed and kept in use. Washington County’s October 2023 water efficiency standards say the same thing about plumbing and the 35-gallon average. If you are buying or building in Washington County, those two documents are the ones to read. We do not track your gallons for you.

Salt Lake discharge, and two winters

Salt Lake City’s pretreatment program requires a questionnaire, and may require a discharge permit, for car and truck washes sending non-domestic wastewater to the sewer. That is a paperwork fact for a tunnel or in-bay that discharges process water.

Along the Wasatch Front, winter means freeze protection, door and heater equipment, ice at the exit, and salt and grit in the reclaim and the pit. St. George’s problem is desert heat and the gallons rule above, not a Wasatch winter. A maintenance rhythm has to follow the site. We do not sell a named winter package.

FAQ

What is St. George’s 35-gallon car wash rule?

St. George City Code section 10-17A-15 says car washes must be plumbed for wash-water and RO-reject recycling at construction. From July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2027, the code says no car wash is permitted unless it averages 35 gallons or less of culinary water per car, and recycling systems must be installed and used. Washington County’s water efficiency standards describe the same plumbing and the same 35-gallon average. Read both before you design or buy.

Do Salt Lake City car washes need a wastewater permit?

Salt Lake City’s pretreatment program says car and truck washes that discharge non-domestic wastewater must submit a Commercial and Industrial User Questionnaire and may need a discharge permit. Ask the city. We do not file it for you from this page.

How do I keep a Utah wash running in winter?

Wasatch Front winters load doors, heaters, exit ice, and salt and grit in the pit. St. George is a desert heat problem more than a deep-freeze problem. We do not publish a winterizing package or a response time. On-site work is by arrangement.
